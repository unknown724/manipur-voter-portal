Statutory Fair Use & Legal Framework

1. Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (Section 52(1)(a)): This platform constitutes fair dealing for non-commercial academic research, private study, criticism, and public civic awareness. No commercial licensing, advertisement, or monetized access is conducted.

2. Public Domain Electoral Rolls: All voter data originates from the officially gazetted Special Summary Revision (SSR 2027) rolls published for open inspection by the Chief Electoral Officer (CEO), Manipur and the Election Commission of India (ECI).

3. Non-Governmental Disclaimer: This portal is an independent academic research utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Election Commission of India. Official enrollment and voter service requests should always be directed to voters.eci.gov.in.

4. Privacy & Data Security: Queries are executed strictly for information assistance and kinship visualization. No personal credentials, biometric identifiers, or user tracking cookies are collected or shared.
